Fishburg

A relaxing aquarium game about fish care in a real-time 3D tank. Buy fish eggs, watch them hatch and raise each fish from baby to adult. Feed them on time, wipe the glass clean with the sponge and sell grown fish for coins and XP. Then decorate your aquarium your way and visit your friends' tanks.

Coming soon as “Fishburg: Aquarium” to the App Store (iPhone and iPad) and Google Play (Android phones and tablets)

  • Fish care at your own pace22 fish species, from fast growers ready in minutes to rare fish that take days, with extra colour variants. Four foods, love for XP, breeding and names for your fish. Time keeps running while the game is closed.
  • Decorate your 3D aquarium34 decorations and 22 plants, including living decor like a hermit crab, a baby octopus and a starfish, plus 9 water scenes and 10 kinds of sand. Move, rotate, flip and resize everything in Edit Mode.
  • Up to 5 tanksEach tank has its own fish, decor and name.
  • Play with friendsAdd friends by player ID, visit their tanks, like them and clean their glass. There is no chat, and you can delete your account at any time.
  • Goals and rewards50 levels that each unlock something new, daily tasks, a reward chest, a free gift, a login calendar and 40 Awards in 8 tracks.
  • No sign-upNo email and no password. Your aquarium is saved on your device, with a backup copy on our server. Optional reminders can each be turned off.

Plays in landscape on phones and tablets, in English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Brazilian Portuguese. Fishburg contains ads and in-app purchases. Remove Ads turns off the automatic full-screen ads; watching a rewarded ad is always your choice.
