## 2. Free puzzles and diamonds

You can open up to 3 new level puzzles for free each day; the daily puzzle is free. Diamonds are an in-game item you earn by playing, or that you can buy as consumable diamond packages (see "Diamond purchases" below). You can use diamonds to open more puzzles or to unlock colour themes: the default, pink and blackberry themes are free, and two more themes can be unlocked with diamonds. ## 3. Diamond purchases

Diamond packages are consumable in-app purchases: virtual items with no cash value, usable only inside the app. Once a diamond package is delivered, it is spent as you use it and is non-refundable, except where required by law or through Apple's own refund process — refund requests are made to Apple, not to us, since Apple handles all payment. Purchased diamonds join the same balance and transaction history as diamonds earned by playing, and are used for the same things (extra puzzles, themes, streak repair).

## 4. Remove Ads and Restore Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that permanently removes the interstitial ads described in "Advertising" below; the optional rewarded ad on the home screen and in the diamond shop stays available afterward, since you choose to watch it. Because Remove Ads is non-consumable, Apple lets you restore it at any time with the app's "Restore Purchases" option, including after reinstalling the app or on a new device signed in with the same Apple ID — no new payment is taken. Diamond packages are consumable, and Apple does not support restoring a consumable purchase through "Restore Purchases"; if iCloud sync is on (see our Privacy Policy), your diamond balance and transaction history are kept and carried over to a reinstall on the same iCloud account. If iCloud sync is off, diamonds and other local data are lost when you delete the app, the same as before purchases existed.

## 5. Streaks and repair

A missed daily puzzle can be repaired with diamonds — 30 diamonds per missed day, within the repair window shown in the app — using diamonds from either source above. A repaired day is not scored and does not count toward the daily leaderboard.
