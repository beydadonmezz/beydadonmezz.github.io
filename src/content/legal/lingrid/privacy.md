This policy explains how the LINGRID mobile app ("the app") and its developer, Beyda Dönmez ("we", "us"), handle information. The app has no account system and no servers of our own. It uses advertising and consent services from Google, and purchases, iCloud sync and Game Center from Apple, each under the terms described below.

## 1. Who is responsible

Beyda Dönmez, the developer and publisher of the app, is responsible for the app and for this policy. You can reach us at [lingrid.sandbox@outlook.com](mailto:lingrid.sandbox@outlook.com).

## 2. Data stored only on your device

To make the game work, the app stores the following on your device, in the app's own storage: your puzzle progress and best scores, daily puzzle results and streak, your diamond balance and its transaction history, whether you have purchased Remove Ads, which colour themes you have unlocked, the free-puzzle allowance for the day, puzzles you have started, and your sound, music, colour theme and appearance settings. This data does not leave your device through the app, except as described in "iCloud sync" below, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you delete the app, unless a copy has been kept in your iCloud account through iCloud sync. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup) may also include it under Apple's control.

## 3. Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app sends the following to Apple so that leaderboards and achievements work: your daily puzzle score together with that day's puzzle number, your all-time total score, and your achievement progress. Your Game Center nickname and scores can be visible to other players on leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); you can manage or turn it off in your device's Settings. The app uses your Game Center identity only to show whether you are signed in; it does not store it or send it anywhere else.

## 4. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

The app may show a short, full-screen interstitial ad between puzzles — when you leave the results screen of a puzzle you have just finished — but never while you are actively solving one. It also offers an optional "rewarded" ad, on the home screen and in the diamond shop, shown only when you choose to watch it, in exchange for diamonds. If you buy Remove Ads, the between-puzzle interstitial ads stop; the rewarded ad stays available because you choose to watch it. These ads are served by Google's Mobile Ads SDK. To request, show and measure them, Google and its advertising partners may process: an approximate, coarse location estimated from your IP address; non-user performance data such as app launch time, crash reports and hang rate, which is used for diagnostics and may also be used to help decide which ads to show, including by sharing it with other companies that display ads; a device identifier — your device's advertising identifier where allowed, or another app- or developer-bound identifier — used for third-party advertising, our own advertising and analytics; and information about which ads you have seen and how you use the app, such as app launches. This list is Google's own disclosure of what its SDK can collect, not our choice; see ["How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services"](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Before showing ads to a user in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message (the "UMP" form) so you can choose whether to allow personalised advertising.

## 5. App Tracking Transparency and non-personalised ads

On iOS, before the app first requests an ad it asks for your permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, shown in Turkish and in English. If you decline tracking, if you have turned tracking off for the app in your device's Settings, or if UMP consent has not been given, the app does not use your device's advertising identifier for tracking and only requests non-personalised ads — ads chosen without a profile built from your activity in other apps or websites. Non-personalised ads still use the Google Mobile Ads SDK data described above (for example, coarse location and a non-tracking device identifier), because Google's SDK needs that data to request, serve, measure and protect any ad, personalised or not. You can change your ATT choice at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; where the EEA/UK/Switzerland consent message applies, you can change your choice from the link Google provides inside that message.

## 6. Purchases through Apple

The app offers in-app purchases — a one-time Remove Ads purchase and consumable diamond packs. All purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit); we do not run our own store and we never receive your payment details, card number or billing address — Apple processes those under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app only receives confirmation of which product you bought and that the purchase succeeded, and finishes each transaction on your device. See our Terms of Use for what each purchase gives you and how Restore Purchases works.

## 7. iCloud sync (Apple)

When iCloud is turned on for the app on your device, the app uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage, part of your own iCloud account, to keep the following in sync across your devices and through a reinstall on the same iCloud account: your diamond balance and its transaction history, whether you have purchased Remove Ads, which colour themes you have unlocked, and your daily streak. This storage is operated by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); we have no server of our own and cannot see this data. If iCloud is off, or you are signed out of iCloud, this data stays only on your device as described in "Data stored only on your device" above, and is lost if you delete the app. To stop this syncing, open your device's Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, and turn iCloud off for the app (or turn off iCloud Drive generally).

## 8. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, precise location or contacts. It asks for permission to send notifications only if you choose daily reminders (below). It asks for permission to use your device's advertising identifier under Apple's App Tracking Transparency, only to support the advertising described above; you can decline this at any time (see "App Tracking Transparency and non-personalised ads").

Daily reminders (optional). The app never asks for notification permission when you first open it. After you finish your first daily puzzle, it asks in its own words whether you would like a reminder; only if you agree does it show Apple's notification permission prompt. Reminders are local notifications: the app schedules them on your device, at most two a day (one at the time you choose and, if you have a streak of two days or more, one later that evening), only for days whose daily puzzle you have not solved yet. No server is involved, no push token is created, and nothing about your reminders or your play is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn reminders off or change their time in Settings (Ayarlar) at any time, and you can withdraw the permission in iOS Settings.

## 9. Network

All puzzles, sounds and images are included in the app. The app connects to the internet for: Apple Game Center, as described above; Google's advertising and consent services, to request and show ads and to gather advertising consent; and Apple's App Store/StoreKit and iCloud services, for purchases and sync. The app shows these legal texts without a connection. When you open their web version, your browser loads it from GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., which processes technical request data such as your IP address to deliver the page. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

## 10. Children

LINGRID is a general-audience word-learning game; we do not direct its marketing at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A user who has not given tracking consent, of any age, is only ever shown non-personalised ads, as described above. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

## 11. Your rights

We hold no personal data of our own beyond what is described above, so there is little for us to access, correct or delete directly. You can delete the data stored on your device by deleting the app; this does not delete a copy kept in your iCloud account, which you control from your device's Settings app under your name, then iCloud. For your Apple ID, Game Center or purchase data, contact Apple. For the advertising data Google's SDK has processed about you, use Google's own controls at myadcenter.google.com, or the choices described in "App Tracking Transparency and non-personalised ads" above. You can still write to us with any privacy question.

## 12. Additional information for users in Türkiye under the Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK)

Under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu, "KVKK"), the data controller (veri sorumlusu) responsible for the personal data described in this policy is Beyda Dönmez, the app's developer. For any KVKK-related request, write to [lingrid.sandbox@outlook.com](mailto:lingrid.sandbox@outlook.com).

Under KVKK article 5, each purpose above rests on one legal basis. Storing your puzzle progress, wallet, daily free-puzzle allowance and settings on your device, handling in-app purchases through Apple, sending your score and achievements to Game Center when you are signed in, and syncing your data through iCloud when you turn it on, are all necessary for the establishment or performance of the contract under which you use the app and the features you choose (article 5/2(c)) — no separate consent is needed for these. Showing personalised advertising and using your device's advertising identifier for tracking rest on your explicit consent (article 5/1), given or withdrawn through the ATT and UMP consent screens described above. The data Google's Mobile Ads SDK needs to request, serve, measure and protect any ad even when you have not consented to tracking (for example coarse location, a non-tracking device identifier, and diagnostic, performance and crash data) rests on our and Google's legitimate interest in operating and securing the free, ad-supported version of the app, without prejudice to your fundamental rights and freedoms (article 5/2(f)).

Yurt dışına aktarım / transfer abroad: Google (Mobile Ads SDK and UMP) and Apple (App Store/StoreKit, Game Center, iCloud) may process the data described above on servers located outside Türkiye, including in the United States. This transfer takes place under KVKK article 9, as amended by Law No. 7499 with effect from 1 June 2024: for personalised advertising and tracking, the transfer rests on your explicit consent; for purchases, Game Center and iCloud sync, it rests on the transfer being directly related to the establishment or performance of the very service you asked for, one of the exceptional grounds listed in article 9(6). Providing the appropriate safeguards KVKK requires for these transfers (such as their own standard contractual clauses) is Google's and Apple's own responsibility as global processors; we do not operate a server of our own that receives this data.

Under KVKK article 11, you have the right to: (a) learn whether your personal data is being processed; (b) request information about it if it has been processed; (c) learn the purpose of the processing and whether it is used in accordance with that purpose; (ç) know the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom your data is transferred; (d) request correction if it has been processed incompletely or incorrectly; (e) request its deletion or destruction, within the conditions set out in the law; (f) request that any correction, deletion or destruction be notified to the third parties to whom your data was transferred; (g) object to a result against you that arises solely from the automated analysis of your data; and (ğ) claim compensation for damage you suffer because of unlawful processing.

To exercise these rights, write to us at [lingrid.sandbox@outlook.com](mailto:lingrid.sandbox@outlook.com); under KVKK article 13 we handle your request free of charge, as quickly as possible and within 30 days at the latest (a fee may apply only for a request that itself requires an additional cost, at the tariff the Personal Data Protection Board sets). If we reject your request, you find our answer insufficient, or we do not answer in time, KVKK article 14 lets you complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu, kvkk.gov.tr) within 30 days of learning our answer and, in any case, within 60 days of your original request.
