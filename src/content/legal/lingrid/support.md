LINGRID is a crossword game where every clue is a Turkish word and its answer is the English translation.

## Contact

For help, feedback or a bug report, email [lingrid.sandbox@outlook.com](mailto:lingrid.sandbox@outlook.com).

## What to include in a report

Your device model, your iOS version, and the puzzle id (shown on the puzzle screen) or a screenshot of the issue.
