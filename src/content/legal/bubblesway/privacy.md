Effective date: September 2026

## 1. About Bubble Sway

Bubble Sway is a casual arcade game for mobile devices. This policy explains what
happens to information when you play it.

Bubble Sway has no account of its own and no sign-up. There is no chat, no messaging,
and no way to post or share content with other players. The game does use Apple's
Game Center for its Best Score leaderboard and Apple's iCloud for cloud save, both
described in section 5; those are Apple services tied to your Apple Account, not an
account you create with us. Bubble Sway does not ask for access to your location,
camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

This page describes the product. It is not legal advice.

## 2. Information We Collect

Bubble Sway does not ask you for your name, address, email address, phone number or
date of birth, and it does not create an account for you.

The game itself does not send your gameplay data to us, and Bubble Sway does not
operate servers of its own. Network communication can still occur through services
built into the game: the advertising services described in section 4,
Apple's Game Center and iCloud services described in section 5, and Apple's App Store
services, which the game's store functionality may contact — for example when
the game starts or returns to the foreground — as described in section 6.

You may choose a short display name and pick an avatar inside Bubble Sway. That
nickname and avatar are stored on your device as part of your saved progress, and
they may be included in the cloud save described in section 5. Please do not use your
real name.

Separately, if you use the leaderboard, Apple Game Center provides its own player
identity and display name, and your best score is submitted to Apple. That is
described in section 5.

Advertising partners do process technical information in order to deliver ads. That
is described in section 4 rather than here, because it is not information Bubble Sway
asks you for or receives.

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

Your progress is saved on your device using the platform's standard app storage. It
is not uploaded to us, and Bubble Sway does not operate a database of its own for it.
A copy may also be stored in your own Apple iCloud account through Apple's cloud save
service, as described in section 5. Your progress includes:

- Your gold balance, lives and best score
- Which themes and cosmetic items you own and have equipped
- Daily rewards, missions and achievements progress
- Settings such as sound, music and tilt controls
- The display name and avatar you chose, if you chose one
- Records of completed purchases, so that what you bought is not granted twice

On the device, this data is readable only by Bubble Sway. Where a cloud copy exists,
it is held in your own Apple iCloud account under Apple's terms, as described in
section 5.

## 4. Advertising

Bubble Sway shows ads through **Google AdMob**, Google's mobile
advertising service, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

### Ads you choose to watch

Rewarded ads are always optional. You start them yourself, and you receive something
in return:

- **Free Gold** — gold, a limited number of times per day
- **Continue** — carrying on after a run ends
- **Extra lives** — when you have run out

If you do not start one, it does not play.

### Ads shown between runs

Bubble Sway also shows occasional full-screen ads between runs. These are not shown
during play, and they are not shown to players who own Bubble Sway PRO or Remove Ads.

### What advertising partners process

To deliver and measure an ad, these services may process technical information from
the device, which can include device and operating system details, coarse region,
network information, and identifiers used for functions such as counting how often an
ad has been shown and detecting fraud. Bubble Sway does not receive this information
and does not have access to it.

Depending on your region and the choices you make, the ads you see may be
personalised - chosen using information such as your interactions with ads and apps -
or non-personalised, which are based on context such as the game itself and your
general location. What Google does with this information is governed by its own
policies, which are linked in section 8.

Bubble Sway does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not ask for
permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

### Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Bubble
Sway uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personal
data is used for advertising. You can consent, decline, or manage your choices, and the
game plays the same whichever you choose; if you decline, you may still see ads that
do not use your personal data. You can review or change your choice at any time from
the Privacy Policy option in the game's Settings, which opens your ad privacy choices
whenever Google's consent tools say you should have that option.

## 5. Game Center and iCloud

### Leaderboard

Bubble Sway uses **Apple Game Center** for its Best Score leaderboard.
Game Center is Apple's service, and signing in to it is handled by Apple on your
device — Bubble Sway does not operate its own account system for it and never
sees your Apple Account credentials.

When Game Center is available and you are signed in, your **best score is
submitted to Apple Game Center**, and your Game Center player identity and
display name may be shown in leaderboard functionality. That information is provided
and handled by Apple under Apple's privacy terms, not by us.

If you are not signed in to Game Center, the leaderboard simply does not submit or
show your score.

### Cloud save

Bubble Sway uses Apple's **GameKit / iCloud Saved Games** service so
that your progress can be restored and kept in step across your own devices. When
this is available, a copy of your Bubble Sway profile — the saved progress
listed in section 3 — may be stored in **your own Apple iCloud
account**.

This data is held by Apple under Apple's terms. **Bubble Sway does not operate
a separate cloud database for it**, does not copy it to any server of ours,
and does not share it with the advertising services in section 4.

Game Center and iCloud are managed through your device's own Settings, where they can
be turned off.

## 6. In-App Purchases

Bubble Sway offers optional purchases — gold packs, Bubble Sway PRO and Remove
Ads. These are handled by **Apple's In-App Purchase system** through
Apple's StoreKit framework.

The game's store functionality uses Apple's StoreKit framework, which may communicate
with Apple's App Store services — for example to check which products are
available — even before any purchase is offered or made.

**Payment is processed by Apple, not by us. Bubble Sway never receives or
stores your payment card details, billing address or store account
credentials.** What the game records is limited to which products you own and
which purchases have already been credited.

What you have bought is an entitlement held by Apple against your store account.
Bubble Sway asks Apple's purchase system to confirm it, and the Restore Purchases
option in the Shop restores non-consumable purchases — Bubble Sway PRO and
Remove Ads — through Apple. Gold packs are consumable and are credited when
bought rather than restored.

Purchases are optional. The game is fully playable without them.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Bubble Sway is a casual game for a general audience. It is not designed for or
directed to children, and it is not listed in the App Store's Kids category.

The game does not ask anyone for personal information, has no chat or messaging
features, and does not ask for permission to track across other apps and websites.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The Best Score leaderboard uses Apple Game Center, and cloud save uses Apple iCloud,
as described in section 5. Both depend on the Apple Account signed in on the device
and can be turned off in your device's Settings. Where Game Center is restricted or
signed out, Bubble Sway simply plays without it.

Parents and guardians: purchases are real-money transactions handled by the platform
store. Your device's own parental controls — Screen Time on iOS, for example
— can restrict or require approval for them.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please
contact us using section 13 and we will look into it.

The advertising services described in section 4 may still measure ads as part of

processing is carried out by those partners under their own policies.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your saved game data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed.
Deleting Bubble Sway removes it. Your device may also offer a way to clear an app's
data without uninstalling it.

Where a cloud copy exists, it is kept in your own Apple iCloud account. Because it is
your iCloud data rather than ours, it is managed through Apple — iCloud storage
settings on your device let you review and remove saved game data, and turning off
iCloud or Game Center stops further syncing. Scores submitted to Game Center are
likewise held by Apple.

Because Bubble Sway has no accounts of its own and no servers, there is no profile
held by us to delete. Information processed by advertising partners is held by those
partners, and their policies in section 8 describe your options.

Purchases are recorded by the platform store under your store account, not by us.

## 10. Security

Game data is stored using the operating system's standard app storage, which other
apps cannot read. Bubble Sway has no account system and no password of its own, so
there is no Bubble Sway login to compromise. Game Center and iCloud are secured by
Apple as part of your Apple Account.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee
absolute security.

## 11. International Users

Bubble Sway may be played anywhere it is distributed. The third-party services listed
in section 8 operate internationally and may process information in countries other
than your own, under the terms of their own policies.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal information held about
you. Because Bubble Sway holds none itself, such requests are best directed to the
relevant service in section 8. You may also contact us and we will help where we can.
